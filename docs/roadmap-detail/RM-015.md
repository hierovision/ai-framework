Define how a consumer repo keeps per-project overrides (stack-specific council-lens bodies, per-project model bindings, project conventions, custom agents) while framework-owned text flows through updates — ending the shadow-copy merge treadmill (pt's council shadows drifted silently; first observed in the 2026-09-25 pt exercise, captured as the shadow-staleness candidate in its exercise file). Acceptance: a consumer can rebase its local agents onto a framework update without hand-diffing whole files; a shadow that is pure framework text is detectable (and droppable) mechanically. **Trigger**: fires with the RM-012 pilot — designed from real exercise evidence, not speculatively.
